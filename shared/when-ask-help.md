# When to ask for help

## Overview

Even experienced users hit issues. Asking for help at the right time, in the right way, gets your problem fixed faster.

## When to reach out

- Your jobs keep failing and you don't know why.
- Software or modules don't load on the node type you need.
- File transfers fail or are very slow.
- You want advice on submitting jobs, managing storage or setting up your software.

## How to get assistance

- **Support tickets**: [iacs.supportsystem.com](https://iacs.supportsystem.com/)
- **Virtual office hours**: for help right away. See [Events](https://rci.stonybrook.edu/events) for the current times.
- **Self-service**: check these guides and the FAQs first. Many common problems are already answered there.

## What to include in a ticket

- Node type and queue used
- Exact commands or scripts executed
- Error messages or log output
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted

!!! tip ""
    **Tip:** the more detail you give, and the faster you answer our questions, the faster we can fix the problem.
