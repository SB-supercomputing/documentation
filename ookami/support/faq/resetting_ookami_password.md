# How to Reset Your Ookami Password

!!! note archive "Archived page"
    Ookami reached end of life at the end of 2025. This is a copy of its website, kept for reference; some original material is not included.

In order to change your password to login to the Ookami cluster, you must reset your NetID password through DoIT. A knowledge base article on this issue can be found [here](https://it.stonybrook.edu/services/netid). Note that changes to your NetID password are campus-wide and will impact other University provided services such as Blackboard, Brightspace, e-mail and Wi-Fi.
