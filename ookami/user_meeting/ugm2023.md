# Ookami

!!! note archive "Archived page"
    Ookami reached end of life at the end of 2025. This is a copy of its website, kept for reference; some original material is not included.

![header](../../assets/ookami/_images/header.png)

The second Ookami user group meeting took place on Thursday, **March 23rd, 2 pm EST.**
The purpose of this meeting is to share experiences, learn from each other, get feedback and have a fruitful discussion about A64FX.
Each slot contains a 15 minute presentation and 5 minutes for Q&A.
