# Kitsune

Kitsune is the Stony Brook Supercomputing Center's new supercomputer, funded by the National Science Foundation, and comes online in the first quarter of 2027. These are the planned specifications SBSC has published. They will be confirmed when the hardware is installed.

<dl class="sbsc-specs">
  <dt>Compute nodes</dt><dd>162</dd>
  <dt>Processors</dt><dd>2x AMD EPYC 9965 (Zen 5) per node</dd>
  <dt>Cores</dt><dd>384 per node; 60,000+ in all</dd>
  <dt>Memory</dt><dd>768 to 1,500 GB per node; 126 TB in all</dd>
  <dt>Peak performance</dt><dd>6.7 PFLOPS, double precision</dd>
  <dt>Storage</dt><dd>6 PB usable GPFS</dd>
  <dt>Interconnect</dt><dd>InfiniBand NDR-400</dd>
  <dt>Operating system</dt><dd>Rocky Linux 10</dd>
  <dt>Coming online</dt><dd>Q1 2027</dd>
  <dt>Allocations</dt><dd>NSF ACCESS, after initial testing</dd>
</dl>

Funded by the National Science Foundation under award OAC-2505376.
