# SeaWulf

SeaWulf is Stony Brook's general-purpose computing cluster, run by Research Computing and Informatics (RCI) and open to the campus community and Brookhaven Lab staff.

<dl class="sbsc-specs">
  <dt>Compute nodes</dt><dd>228</dd>
  <dt>CPU cores</dt><dd>18,152</dd>
  <dt>Processors</dt><dd>Intel Skylake, AMD EPYC Milan, Intel Sapphire Rapids (HBM), Intel Ice Lake</dd>
  <dt>GPUs</dt><dd>44 NVIDIA A100 (80 GB), 4 per node</dd>
  <dt>Memory</dt><dd>192 GB to about 1 TB per node</dd>
  <dt>Storage</dt><dd>GPFS: home 20 GB, scratch 20 TB, project up to 10 TB</dd>
  <dt>Patient data</dt><dd>Not approved for PHI</dd>
  <dt>Status</dt><dd>In service</dd>
</dl>

The [SeaWulf cluster overview](../seawulf/README.md) covers each node type, the queues, and how to connect.

Source: RCI's [SeaWulf page](https://rci.stonybrook.edu/hpc/clusters/seawulf) and [storage layout and policies](https://rci.stonybrook.edu/docs/storage-layout-policies), checked November 19, 2026.
