# NVwulf

NVwulf is Stony Brook's GPU cluster for AI and machine learning, run by Research Computing and Informatics (RCI) and open to Stony Brook instructors, researchers, staff, and students.

<dl class="sbsc-specs">
  <dt>GPU nodes</dt><dd>20</dd>
  <dt>GPUs</dt><dd>96: 48 NVIDIA H200 and 48 RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell</dd>
  <dt>GPU memory</dt><dd>141 GB HBM3e per H200; 96 GB GDDR7 per RTX PRO 6000</dd>
  <dt>GPUs per node</dt><dd>4 or 8</dd>
  <dt>CPUs per node</dt><dd>64 to 192</dd>
  <dt>Memory</dt><dd>About 512 GB to 2,200 GB per node</dd>
  <dt>Storage</dt><dd>Lustre, separate from SeaWulf: home 20 GB, scratch 20 TB</dd>
  <dt>Scheduler</dt><dd>Slurm, up to 8 GPUs per node</dd>
  <dt>In service</dt><dd>Since June 2025, expanded through fall 2026</dd>
</dl>

The [NVwulf cluster overview](../nvwulf/README.md) covers each node type, the queues, and how to connect.

Source: RCI's [NVwulf page](https://rci.stonybrook.edu/hpc/clusters/nvwulf) and [HPC by the numbers](https://rci.stonybrook.edu/hpc/by-the-numbers), checked November 19, 2026.
