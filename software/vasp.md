# VASP

A software package for atomic-scale materials modeling using quantum mechanical simulations.

VASP is a highly efficient software package used for performing quantum mechanical simulations in materials science. It allows users to study the properties of materials using density functional theory (DFT), and is widely used in research for simulations involving atomic structures, electronic properties, and more. Access to VASP is restricted and requires a valid license.

**Module name:** `vasp`

## Access & licensing

> [!NOTE]
> **License-restricted: no shared module.** VASP is not available as a standard shared module on SeaWulf. You (or your PI) must hold an active VASP license.

To get access:

1. Confirm that you or your Principal Investigator holds an active VASP license.
2. Contact HPC support with your license verification.
3. RCI installs VASP in a private location for your group’s use once the license is verified.

## About VASP

VASP (the Vienna Ab initio Simulation Package) performs quantum-mechanical simulations for materials science, such as density functional theory (DFT) studies across solid-state physics, chemistry, and materials research, including atomic structures and electronic properties.

**Category:** Molecular dynamics & chemistry
