# Ookami

Ookami was the Stony Brook Supercomputing Center's Fujitsu A64FX testbed, funded by the National Science Foundation. It reached end of life at the end of 2025, and its website is kept here as an archive.

<dl class="sbsc-specs">
  <dt>System</dt><dd>HPE (formerly Cray) Apollo 80</dd>
  <dt>Compute nodes</dt><dd>176 Fujitsu A64FX, including 2 for debugging</dd>
  <dt>Cores</dt><dd>48 per node</dd>
  <dt>Memory</dt><dd>32 GB high-bandwidth memory per node</dd>
  <dt>Peak performance</dt><dd>2.76 TFLOPS per CPU, double precision</dd>
  <dt>Local storage</dt><dd>512 GB SSD per node</dd>
  <dt>Shared storage</dt><dd>About 800 TB Lustre</dd>
  <dt>Interconnect</dt><dd>InfiniBand</dd>
  <dt>Other nodes</dt><dd>1 AMD Milan, 2 Thunder X2, 1 Intel Skylake, 2 NVIDIA Grace</dd>
  <dt>Allocations</dt><dd>NSF ACCESS, from October 2022</dd>
  <dt>Status</dt><dd>Retired at the end of 2025</dd>
</dl>

Funded by the National Science Foundation under award OAC-1927880.

The archived site starts at the [Ookami home page](../ookami/README.md), and its user guide is the [FAQ](../ookami/support/faq/README.md).

Source: the archived Ookami website ([home page](../ookami/README.md), [What is Ookami?](../ookami/support/faq/what_is_ookami.md), and [Infiniband Topology](../ookami/support/faq/infiniband_topology.md)), saved November 5, 2026, and [Kitsune on the SBSC website](https://sbsc.stonybrook.edu/resources/kitsune), which describes Ookami as SBSC's previous NSF-funded resource.
